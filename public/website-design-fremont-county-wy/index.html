Why a Fremont County website is not a generic Wyoming template

Fremont County has a mix of markets. Riverton is the commercial hub. Lander is the county seat, with outdoor traffic tied to Sinks Canyon and the Wind River Range. Dubois sits on the upper Wind River toward Togwotee Pass. Shoshoni sits at the U.S. 20/26 junction near Boysen Reservoir. Hudson sits on the road between Riverton and Lander. The Wind River Indian Reservation covers a large part of the county. People search in all of those places for contractors, shops, clinics, lodges, and services that will come to them.

A national template that drops “Fremont County” into the same paragraph used for fifty other counties does not help those searches. Neither does a pretty site with a buried phone number. Customers on a phone need to know what you do, where you work, and how to reach you in a few taps.

We write and design for that reality. We are not a remote agency guessing at the map. Home base is Riverton, WY. When a project needs a face-to-face kickoff, we can meet in the county instead of scheduling around another time zone.
