What tattoo shops need from a website

Artists sell trust and style. The site should show work, explain how to book, and set expectations.

  • Portfolio galleries by artist or style
  • Booking / consult forms with idea, size, placement, and budget range fields you want
  • Deposit and prep policies stated plainly
  • Artist pages with booking links
  • Hours and walk-in vs. appointment rules
  • Google Business Profile photos and categories
  • Age / ID policy reminders without turning the site into legal advice
  • Mobile-first galleries that still load reasonably

Searches include “tattoo shop Riverton,” “tattoo near me,” “fine line tattoo Jackson,” “tattoo studio Casper.” Tourists often decide same-day from Maps.
