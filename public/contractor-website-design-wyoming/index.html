What contractors need from a website

A contractor site has one job: turn a search into a call or a quote request. Most weak sites fail the same way.

  • Services up front — concrete, roofing, remodels, excavation — named the way customers search, not buried under stock photos
  • Service areas that match the trucks — Riverton, Lander, Dubois, Shoshoni, Hudson, and the towns you actually cover
  • Click-to-call and a short quote form above the fold on phones
  • Real project photos of your work, not generic templates
  • Licensing, insurance, and local trust cues that are easy to find
  • Google Business Profile alignment so Maps and your website say the same thing
  • Reviews that show up where buyers look before they call
  • Call tracking so you know which pages and searches create real estimate requests

People search phrases like “concrete contractor Riverton WY,” “roofing company near me,” “home builder Lander,” “excavation Fremont County,” and “remodel contractor Casper.” Your pages should answer those searches with clear services, towns, and a next step.
