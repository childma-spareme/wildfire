What senior living sites need to do

Family researchers compare care levels, amenities, location, and how easy it is to request a tour.

  • Care levels explained plainly — only what you actually offer
  • Tour / inquiry forms with room for family contact and timing
  • Photo and video tours of real common spaces and grounds
  • Floor plans or residence types when you share them
  • Staff and community story without invented testimonials
  • Google Business Profile accuracy for Maps and reviews
  • Resource FAQs for families (move-in basics, visiting, pets) — not legal or medical advice
  • Mobile-friendly layout for adult children researching at night

Searches include “assisted living Riverton WY,” “retirement community Lander,” “senior living near me,” “memory care Casper.” Keep language accurate and respectful.
