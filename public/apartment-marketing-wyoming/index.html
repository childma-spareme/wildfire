What apartment sites need to do

Renters want what’s available, what it costs to inquire, pet policy, and a tour path — updated honestly.

  • Availability / vacancy cues you can update without drama
  • Floor plans and amenity lists
  • Tour request forms with move-in timing fields
  • Pet, parking, and utility notes
  • Photo galleries of real units and grounds (not only one staged unit forever)
  • Google Business Profile accuracy for Maps
  • Application or “how to apply” links to your process
  • Mobile-first layout for renters searching after work

Searches include “apartments Riverton WY,” “2 bedroom apartment Lander,” “pet friendly apartments Casper,” “housing Rock Springs.” Keep availability truthful.
