Lander is not Riverton with a different headline

Lander is Fremont County’s seat. Government offices, outdoor recreation around Sinks Canyon, education and guiding tied to the Wind River Range, healthcare and professional services, builders and remodelers, and Main Street shops all share the same small downtown grid — and the same Google map pack when someone searches from a phone.

Riverton, twenty-five minutes down the road, is the county’s commercial hub. The two towns overlap on some trades and “near me” searches, especially along the corridor through Hudson. They do not overlap on everything. A Lander SEO plan that is just a find-and-replace of a Riverton page will miss how people actually look for outfitters, clinics, contractors, and local services here.

What Lander local SEO includes

  • Google Business Profile — categories, services, photos, posts, and review reply habits that match how you sell in Lander. Service areas should include Lander and the other towns you truly cover
  • Website local structure — a Lander website or Lander pages with consistent name, city, and phone. Tap-to-call belongs near the top of the page, not only in the footer
  • On-page SEO — titles, headings, and internal links that match Lander and corridor searches without stuffing
  • Listings and citations — cleanup so directories do not send people to an old number or the wrong town
  • Reviews — an honest request process after good jobs. We never use fake reviews. See Google reviews
  • Tracking — calls and form leads you can actually see, including phone-call events from tap-to-call links on the site

We do not promise a specific map position. The audit tells you what is in the way and what we would do first.
