What food trucks need from a website

The site should answer where, when, what, and how to book you for an event — fast.

  • “Where are we today” block that’s easy to update
  • Weekly schedule for regular stops
  • Menu readable on phones
  • Catering / event inquiry form with date and headcount fields
  • Google Business Profile posts and photos for daily locations when appropriate
  • Facebook/Instagram links without making social the only source of truth
  • Service area notes for catering travel
  • Click-to-call for same-day questions

Searches include “food truck Riverton,” “food truck near me,” “catering food truck Lander,” “job site lunch truck Casper.” Energy towns often want predictable stop schedules.
