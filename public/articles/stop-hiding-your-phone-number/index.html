Stop hiding your phone number on your website

Why contractors lose mobile leads when the phone number is buried — and how call-first website design fixes it.

Call 307-240-8720 · Request a free audit

Call 307-240-8720 All articles

If a customer has to hunt for your number, many will tap the competitor who put Call in their face. That is especially true for trades — people search when something is broken, leaking, or on a deadline.

What “hiding” looks like

  • Number only in a tiny footer
  • Number as a non-tappable image or graphic
  • Contact page with a form and no phone
  • Click-to-chat widgets that bury the call option

What we do instead

On sites we build for Wyoming contractors and small businesses, the phone number is visible early, tappable on mobile, and repeated near major calls to action. The header and hero usually include a tap-to-call path. Forms still matter for after-hours — they do not replace the number.

Pair the site with Maps

Even a perfect call button loses if nobody finds you. Local SEO and a strong Google Business Profile bring the right searches; the site closes the loop. Area pages like Riverton, Lander, Dubois, Shoshoni, and Hudson help you speak to the towns you serve.
