Launch is not the finish line

Most small-business sites in Riverton, Lander, and across Wyoming are built, celebrated, and then ignored until something breaks. By then the damage is already done: a customer tried a dead form, landed on last year’s hours, or left because the page took too long on a phone.

Website maintenance is the unglamorous work that prevents that. It is updates and backups so you are not one bad plugin away from a blank page. It is speed and security so the site stays safe and usable. It is a short list of content edits — a new service, a new photo, a seasonal note — so the site still matches how you actually work.

We would rather catch a problem on a Tuesday than hear about it from a customer who already called someone else. That is the point of a maintenance relationship with an owner-operator firm in Riverton, WY, instead of hoping a leftover login still works when you need it.

If the site itself is the wrong shape for calls, maintenance will not fix that alone. In those cases we will say so and point you to website design. Maintenance is for sites worth keeping healthy.

What we watch and update

  • Updates — platform, plugin, and dependency updates applied carefully, not all at once on a Friday night with no backup
  • Backups — regular copies you can restore if an update or a hosting issue goes wrong
  • Speed — we keep an eye on load time, especially on phones, because a slow page loses the same customer your lead generation work is trying to catch
  • Security — basic hardening, SSL, login hygiene, and watching for obvious issues. We will not pretend this is a substitute for a full security audit of a complex app, but most small business sites need this layer kept current
  • Content edits — hours, phone numbers, service lists, photos, and short seasonal notes you send us, so the site does not contradict your Google Business Profile
  • Broken paths — forms, tap-to-call links, and important internal links checked so a quiet failure does not sit for months

The audit is how we decide what is in scope and whether the site is a good candidate for maintenance or needs a rebuild first.

Who website maintenance is for

This service fits Wyoming businesses that already have a site — whether we built it or not — and need someone responsible to keep it current:

  • Contractors and trades who cannot spend evenings logging into a dashboard
  • Main Street shops and local services whose hours and offers change
  • Owner-operators who launched a site last year and have not touched it since
  • Businesses running sites that support local SEO in Riverton, Lander, or the rest of Fremont County, and cannot afford a stale page to undercut that work

If you do not have a site yet, start with design. If you have one that almost works, maintenance may be enough. We will tell you the difference in plain language.

Questions owners ask

Frequently asked questions

What does website maintenance include?

The usual package of work we keep an eye on: software and plugin updates, backups, uptime and basic security, speed checks, broken links, and small content updates you send us — hours, services, photos, seasonal notes. The exact list is agreed on the audit.

We just launched a new site. Do we still need maintenance?

A new site still ages. Plugins and platforms release updates, content goes stale, and forms or phone numbers can break after a quiet month. Maintenance is how we keep the launch from slowly unraveling.

Can you maintain a site you did not build?

Often yes, after we look at how it was built and whether we can work on it safely. Some older sites need a rebuild more than a tune-up. We will tell you which path we recommend before you commit. See website design if a rebuild is the better fix.

Will you change the site without asking?

Not for content or design changes. Routine updates and backups should not alter how the site looks. Anything public-facing we will run by you unless we have already agreed on a standing rule.

Does maintenance include SEO or lead generation?

Maintenance keeps the site healthy. Local SEO and lead generation are separate work, though a neglected site can undermine both. We can combine them when that is what you need.
