Who this is for

  • Youth leagues: baseball, softball, soccer, football, basketball, volleyball, and wrestling
  • Clubs: swim, ski and snowboard, hockey, climbing, running, and rodeo clubs
  • Adult leagues: co-ed softball, volleyball, basketball, pickleball, bowling, and golf leagues
  • Tournaments and camps that need a clear page for teams traveling in
  • Booster groups that support school and community teams

Most leagues are run by volunteer boards and coaches with day jobs. The website should make their season easier, not add more work.

What leagues and clubs need from a website

  • Registration front and center, with dates, age divisions, fees, and deadlines (we link to the registration tool you use or set up a simple form)
  • Online payments through a secure checkout that works on phones
  • Schedules and field locations with maps, plus a quick way to post rainouts, wind delays, and field closures
  • Coach and volunteer sign-ups, including background-check steps if your league requires them
  • Sponsor recognition for local businesses, with logos and links you can update every season
  • Rules, forms, and policies: code of conduct, concussion info, refund policy, and equipment lists
  • Contact info for the right board member, not just a generic inbox
  • Privacy for kids: If your site collects personal information directly from children under 13, the FTC's COPPA rule requires verifiable parental consent first (FTC COPPA FAQ). We design registration so parents provide the information, and we keep player photos and names to what your league has approved.

What we build

  • A home page with what's happening right now: registration open, schedule posted, or games canceled
  • A registration page that explains divisions, fees, deadlines, and scholarships or fee help, then sends families to sign up
  • Schedule and field pages with maps and a weather/cancellation banner your board can switch on from a phone
  • Volunteer and coach sign-up forms sent to the right person
  • A sponsors page and home page logo strip, built to sell and recognize sponsorships
  • Pages for rules, forms, and FAQs so the same questions stop landing in your inbox
  • Photo galleries with a simple, league-approved photo policy
  • A Google Business Profile and basic local search setup so "[town] youth soccer" or "[town] softball league" finds you

Easy to update after handoff

League boards change every year or two, so the site has to work for whoever comes next.

  • Phone-friendly editing: post a rainout from the parking lot in under a minute
  • Season templates: roll last year's registration and schedule pages forward and change the dates
  • Roles: the registrar, scheduler, and sponsor coordinator each get their own login
  • League ownership: the domain, hosting, and payment accounts stay in the league's name
  • A one-page handoff sheet for the next board, with logins, renewal dates, and how-to steps
  • Optional help: we can handle preseason updates or stay on call during the season

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

We already use a registration app. Do we still need a website?

Registration apps handle signups and payments well, but they usually aren't where people land when they search your league on Google. Your website explains the season, answers common questions, recognizes sponsors, and sends families to your registration app at the right time. We work with the tools you already use.

Can we post rainouts and field closures quickly?

Yes. We add a cancellation banner a board member can turn on from a phone in under a minute. It appears at the top of every page until you turn it off.

How do you handle kids' information and photos?

We set registration up so parents provide the information. If a site collects personal information directly from children under 13, the FTC's COPPA rule requires verifiable parental consent first. We also follow your league's photo policy, such as first names only or no photos without permission.

Can the website help us sell sponsorships?

Yes. A sponsors page and a home page logo strip give local businesses real visibility, and a short "Become a sponsor" form makes it easy to ask. You can update sponsors every season.

What happens when our board changes?

The site stays in the league's name. We leave a one-page handoff sheet with logins, renewal dates, and how-to steps, and you can call us to walk new board members through it.

Do you work with adult leagues too?

Yes. Adult rec leagues need many of the same things: team signups, schedules, standings or results, rules, and rainout updates. We keep it simple for captains and players.
