# Wildfire Digital Marketing Design Direction

## Brand Position

Wildfire Digital Marketing serves contractors and small business owners in Fremont County, Wyoming, including Riverton, Lander, Dubois, Shoshoni, Hudson, and nearby communities. The site should feel local, experienced, direct, and practical. The promise is not abstract marketing activity; it is better visibility, a more professional online presence, more leads, and more phone calls.

## Visual System

- Primary text: `#121212`
- Light gray: `#f1f2f4`
- Warm gray: `#e7e8ea`
- Flame orange: `#f05a28`
- Hot orange: `#ff7a1a`
- Warm light: `#fff4ee`
- White: `#ffffff`
- Muted text: `#68625e`

Use light gray and white as the main page backgrounds. Use orange for primary calls-to-action, section accents, metrics, and important labels. Avoid black backgrounds, heavy gradients, and decorative effects that make the business feel generic.

- Website building and management
- Lead generation
- Making the phone ring
- Local search and Google visibility

- Riverton, Lander, Dubois, and Shoshoni SEO visibility

## Design Notes

The site is inspired by the Webflow-style design direction: polished sections, strong spacing, crisp cards, clear CTAs, and high-contrast typography. Cards should stay at 8px border radius or less. The page should feel like a real working business website, not a landing page full of vague marketing claims.
